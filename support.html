Common questions

Is safe to spend my bank balance?

No. Safe to spend is an estimate based on what you enter. It isn't your bank balance and it can't guarantee a purchase will go through. Use Check against your bank in the app to line it up with your bank.

I forgot my backup passphrase.

We can't recover it, and we can't open your backup file. Backups are encrypted with the passphrase you chose.

Does the app connect to my bank?

No. Headroom has no bank connection, no account and no analytics. See the privacy policy.

I don't get paid every two weeks. Does it still work?

Yes. Pick weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly, or enter each next payday yourself.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Your data lives only on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes it. Turn on the weekly encrypted backup (Settings › Data) and you can restore from your Files folder.

Wasn't this app called Spending Tracker?

Yes, until 8 November 2026. Only the name changed. Backups made under the old name restore as usual.
