Support

For help with Spending Tracker, email george@vogas.net.

Please include your iPhone model, the iOS version and a short description of what you were doing. Don't send bank details, account numbers or your backup passphrase.

Common questions

Is safe to spend my bank balance?

No. Safe to spend is an estimate based on what you enter. It isn't your bank balance and it can't guarantee a purchase will go through. Use Check against your bank in the app to line it up with your bank.

I forgot my backup passphrase.

We can't recover it, and we can't open your backup file. Backups are encrypted with the passphrase you chose.

Does the app connect to my bank?

No. Spending Tracker has no bank connection, no account and no analytics. See the privacy policy.
