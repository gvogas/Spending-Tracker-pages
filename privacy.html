Privacy policy

Spending Tracker · last updated 2 November 2026

Spending Tracker helps you see what you can safely spend before payday. It's built so that your financial information stays with you.

What we collect

Nothing. Spending Tracker has no account, no server and no analytics. We don't collect, receive, sell or share any information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter (balances, transactions, notes, bills, paydays, goals and settings) is stored only on your iPhone, protected by iOS's built-in encryption. You can also turn on an app lock that asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

  • Bank connections: none. The app never asks for bank logins and never connects to your bank.
  • Widget: the Home Screen widget shows safe to spend and today's amount. Turn on Hide amounts on Home Screen and the widget shows, and stores, no figures at all.
  • Apple Watch: if you install the Watch app, your iPhone sends it the same short summary the widget uses (safe to spend, today's amount, days to payday), directly to your own watch. No transactions, notes or names are sent, and with Hide amounts on Home Screen on, no figures are sent either.
  • Backups: if you choose Back up now, the app creates a file encrypted with a passphrase you choose and saves it where you pick, for example iCloud Drive. That file is handled by Apple or the storage service you chose, under their privacy terms. We can't open it and we can't recover your passphrase.
  • Spreadsheet export (Pro): creates a file you choose where to save. It isn't encrypted; treat it like any financial document.
  • Receipt scanning (Pro): you take a photo of a receipt, or choose one from your photo library. The text is read on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. The photo is never uploaded or kept, and the camera is used only for this.

Purchases

Spending Tracker Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we only receive confirmation from Apple, on your device, that the purchase is valid. We never see your payment details.

Children

Spending Tracker isn't directed to children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
