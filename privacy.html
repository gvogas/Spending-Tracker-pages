Privacy policy

Headroom · last updated 13 November 2026

Headroom helps you see what you can safely spend before payday. It's built so that your financial information stays with you. Headroom was called Spending Tracker until 8 November 2026; only the name changed, and this policy covers both.

What we collect

In the app, nothing. Headroom has no account, no server and no analytics. We don't collect, receive, sell or share any information about you or how you use the app.

The email list on this website

The website has one optional form. If you join the email list, we keep the email address you type, the kind of phone you chose (if you chose one), which form on the page you used, and the tag on the link that brought you here if it had one (for example ?ref=instagram), so we can tell which posts people came from. The form is handled by Formspree, which receives the submission on our behalf under its own privacy terms.

We use your address only to tell you when Headroom is on the App Store and when there's an update. We don't sell or share it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time at george@vogas.net. The website has no analytics, no cookies and no tracking scripts, and the calculator on the page works in your browser: the figures you type into it are never sent or stored.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter (balances, transactions, notes, bills, paydays, goals and settings) is stored on your iPhone, protected by iOS's built-in encryption. It is never sent to us. You can also turn on an app lock that asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

  • Bank connections: none. The app never asks for bank logins and never connects to your bank.
  • Widget: the Home Screen widget shows safe to spend and today's amount. Turn on Hide amounts on Home Screen and the widget shows, and stores, no figures at all.
  • Apple Watch: if you install the Watch app, your iPhone sends it the same short summary the widget uses (safe to spend, today's amount, days to payday), directly to your own watch. No transactions, notes or names are sent, and with Hide amounts on Home Screen on, no figures are sent either.
  • iPhone backups: like other apps' data, Headroom's data is included when you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer. That backup is made and kept by Apple under your Apple Account, or by you on your computer; we never see it.
  • App lock and the widget: turning on app lock also turns on Hide amounts on Home Screen, so the widget and watch show no figures. You can turn that back off.
  • Backups: if you choose Back up now, the app creates a file encrypted with a passphrase you choose and saves it where you pick, for example iCloud Drive. That file is handled by Apple or the storage service you chose, under their privacy terms. We can't open it and we can't recover your passphrase.
  • Weekly automatic backup (off unless you turn it on): the app writes the same kind of encrypted file, once a week, to a folder in Files that you pick. Your passphrase is kept in your iPhone's Keychain, on this iPhone only (it isn't synced to iCloud Keychain, doesn't travel in device backups and never leaves the device); the app keeps the newest eight files and removes only files it created itself. If the folder is on iCloud Drive or another provider, that provider syncs the encrypted file the way it syncs any other file.
  • Recently deleted: a transaction you delete stays on your iPhone for 30 days so you can bring it back, then it is removed. It is in any backup made during those 30 days. Delete now removes it at once.
  • Reminders: bill reminders, a payday note (Pro) and an optional reminder on days you haven't logged anything are notifications your iPhone schedules for itself. Nothing is sent to us or to any server. The logging reminder never contains an amount, a bill name or a note; it works out your usual time from when you log, never from what you log, and that time isn't stored.
  • Trip Live Activity (Pro): while a trip is on, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island can show the trip's name and daily figure. It is updated by the app on your iPhone, not by a push server. With Hide amounts on Home Screen on it shows no figures, and with app lock on it shows only the trip's name.
  • Widget button (Pro): the medium widget's Log my usual button logs a purchase without opening the app. It runs inside the app on your iPhone; the widget itself never reads your transactions.
  • Share a month (Pro) and the other exports (spreadsheet, monthly PDF, year-end bundle, report card image): the app makes a file or image and hands it to the iPhone's share sheet. You choose what's on it and where it goes. It isn't encrypted; treat it like any financial document. The app itself sends nothing anywhere.
  • Receipt scanning (Pro): you take a photo of a receipt, or choose one from your photo library. The text is read on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. The photo is never uploaded. It is kept with the transaction only if you turn on Keep receipt photos, and it goes when the transaction does.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: what you say to Siri is handled by Apple under Apple's terms. The app's replies never include figures while amounts are hidden or app lock is on.

None of this adds any collection. We still receive nothing.

Purchases

Headroom Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we only receive confirmation from Apple, on your device, that the purchase is valid. We never see your payment details.

Children

Headroom isn't directed to children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
