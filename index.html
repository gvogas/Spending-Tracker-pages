How the number works

Your balance, minus bills and savings, divided by the days to payday.

Safe to spend is your balance minus the bills due before payday and whatever you've set aside. Divided by the days left, it becomes a daily amount.

Questions

Before you download

Is safe to spend my bank balance?

No. It's an estimate from the balance, bills and set-asides you enter, so it's as current as what you've typed. Compare it with your bank now and then, and update the balance if they differ.

I don't get paid every two weeks. Does it still work?

Yes. Pick weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly, or enter each next payday yourself.

Is it in French?

Yes. The app is in English and French (Canada), and every amount is in Canadian dollars.

What do I need?

An iPhone on iOS 26 or later. The Apple Watch app is optional and needs watchOS 26.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Your data lives only on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes it. Turn on the weekly encrypted backup and you can restore it from your Files folder.

How much does it cost?

The app is free. Pro is a one-time CAD 9.99 purchase with no subscription. If a purchase is refunded, Pro turns off and nothing you entered is lost or hidden.
